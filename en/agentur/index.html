Language support for your client projects

You manage the project and client relationship. I support small and midsize agencies behind the scenes with German Easy Language, Plain Language for German factual texts, final editing and language quality assurance.

What I can handle for agencies

I work from your brief, existing content and coordination with your client. Depending on the assignment, you receive a finished text, an edited version with comments or a consolidated client-ready draft in the agreed file format.

  • German Easy Language for websites, information materials and existing client content
  • Plain Language for German factual texts when a less simplified version is the right fit
  • Final editing and quality assurance for client or AI-generated drafts
  • Editing and German-English translation

Why professional language work still matters when using AI

AI can speed up drafting. For a client project, the final version still needs to follow the brief and use the right terminology. Within the agreed scope, I review wording, terminology, inconsistencies, sources and critical statements. You receive a considered, client-ready version, not a legal review or a blanket guarantee that the text is error-free.

For an overview of language levels, see the pages on Easy Language, Plain Language and AI quality assurance.

Working behind the scenes

At the start, we agree on the audience, source material, file format, terminology and contact for questions. I group subject-matter questions so you can resolve them with your client. I deliver in the agreed format, with one revision round included in the agreed scope. Additional rounds or content are agreed separately.

Any review by a target or review group is arranged by the client. I can prepare the text for that stage and incorporate feedback within the agreed scope.

Pricing and project scope

Pricing depends on the file format, complexity, language and deadline. To get an initial assessment, you can send the material or briefly describe the project. The quote sets out the scope, deliverables and revision round. See indicative prices.

Frequently asked questions

What should I include in an inquiry?

A brief, the source text or draft, the desired file format, intended audience, terminology and the agreed route for questions are helpful.

How does the revision round work?

I deliver the agreed version and include one revision round within the scope set in advance. Project questions are grouped and sent through the agreed contacts.

Do you perform technical reviews of websites or PDFs?

No. This page covers language support. For website accessibility analysis, see the analysis service.
