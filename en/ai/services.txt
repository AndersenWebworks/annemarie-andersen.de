Services by Annemarie Andersen

1. Accessibility & Easy Language (core service)
   Texts in Easy Language to the current rulebook. For websites, forms, brochures and accessibility statements. Translation of existing content or new texts from scratch, including source texts in standard language.

2. Clear Administrative Language
   Turning officialese into clear language. Wording notices, forms, contract clauses and terms and conditions so that the target group understands them on first reading.

3. AI Quality Assurance & Post-Editing
   Checking AI-generated texts for factual errors, breaks in style and compliance gaps. For companies that use ChatGPT, DeepL or other tools for their texts.

4. Editing & Translation
   Proofreading, stylistic editing and specialist German-English translation. From technical documentation through marketing copy to print artwork.

5. Copywriting
   Impact reports, annual reports, grant applications and concepts -- written clearly, well structured and tailored to the target group.
