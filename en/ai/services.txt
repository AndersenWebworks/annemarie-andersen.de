Services by Annemarie Andersen

1. Easy Language
   Rewriting existing texts or creating new German texts in Easy Language for websites, forms, brochures and public information. Any target-group or review-group testing is arranged by the client; I can prepare the text and incorporate feedback within the agreed scope.

2. Plain Language (Einfache Sprache)
   Revision and new writing for German factual texts under DIN 8581-1, including customer information, forms, administrative texts, health communication and internal guidelines when Easy Language is not the right level.

3. Language Support for Agencies
   Easy Language, Plain Language, final editing, quality assurance of client and AI drafts, editing and German-English translation for agency client projects.

4. Clear Administrative Language
   Rewriting administrative German in clearer language for notices, forms, contract clauses and terms and conditions. Legal review remains the responsibility of the competent legal professional.

5. AI Quality Assurance and Post-Editing
   Reviewing AI-generated texts for factual errors, source issues, stylistic inconsistencies and compliance-related risks.

6. Editing and Translation
   Proofreading, stylistic editing and specialist German-English translation, from technical documentation to marketing copy.

7. Copywriting
   Impact reports, annual reports, corporate reports, grant applications and concepts, written clearly and tailored to the target audience.

8. Website Accessibility Analysis
   WCAG-oriented review of representative page types, with a written report, prioritized findings and recommendations for technical implementation. No certificate of conformance or legal advice. Compact analysis from EUR 790.
